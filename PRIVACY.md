# Privacy

This site uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Most of it is static pages and a PDF of the manual, which you can read without giving any details.

## Join the network form

The form at [archivingthepresent.cc/join/](https://archivingthepresent.cc/join/) is the only part of the site that collects personal data.

- It asks for your name, email, and optionally a phone number. It also asks how you want to join, your language community or organisation, short bios, links, areas of knowledge, and whether you want to be listed in the network directory.
- The form sends your answers through Google Apps Script, a Google service. They are stored in a Google Sheet that only OpenSpeaks representatives, who organise the network, can open. [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies to this data.
- If your request is approved, you receive a member agreement as a PDF. It is made with Google Docs and sent with Gmail.
- Your details are used only for the network. They are not sold or shared.
- Your name and details appear in the network directory only if you agree to that on the form.
- To see, correct or remove your details, or to leave the network, write to hello@archivingthepresent.cc.
