Network

Organised by

    A community network helping Indigenous and marginalised-language communities across South Asia document and archive their own knowledge.

Network Collaborators

Archiving the Present grew out of the OpenSpeaks Community Language Documentation and Archiving workshop series. It launched in person as a workshop by the same name, alongside WikiConference India, Kochi, on 3 September 2026, celebrating Creative Commons' 25th anniversary. Groups, collectives, and organisations that contributed in its early conception in and beyond the workshop include Adivasi Lives Matter, Adiwasi Janjagruti, Archival and Research Project (ARPO), Design Beku, Endangered Languages Archive (ELAR), Endangered Languages Project, FOSS United, Global Voices, Janastu, Open Knowledge Initiatives (OKI-IIITH), People’s Archive of Rural India (PARI), Rising Voices, Santali Art & Film Development Foundation, Whose Knowledge?, and Wikimedians of Kerala User Group. Participants also documented Allahabadi-Awadhi, Angika, Badi, Balesoria-Odia, Bangla, Damai, Dangani, Gandharva, Garhwali, Gondi, Hindi, Ho, Juray, Kami, Koda, Kurukh, Lanjia-Sora, Mahli, Mavli, Mundari, Sadri, Santali, Sarki, Tamil, and Van Gujjari. We are reaching out to these and other groups now to confirm listing.

Funding

Fundraising is a skill, like recording or archiving, and community documenters can learn it too. A few funders that support language documentation and archiving work directly:

  • Endangered Languages Documentation Programme (ELDP)

    Grants for language documentation fieldwork worldwide, plus Legacy Material Grants for digitising old recordings.

  • Firebird Foundation for Anthropological Research

    Grants up to $10,000, including a supplemental grant for oral literature and traditional ecological knowledge. Deadlines fall on 15 January and 15 June each year.

  • Endangered Language Fund

    Language Legacies grants, open to community members and researchers from any country.

  • Foundation for Endangered Languages

    Small grants, around $1,000, for documentation and revitalisation projects.

Compiled from Module 8, Fundraising, by Mandana Seyfeddinipur.
