Join the network

Use this form to join as a representative of a language community, an organisation, or both. It takes about five minutes.

How would you like to join? (required) Choose one or both.

Why do you want to be part of this network?
Can we list your name, language community or communities, and areas of knowledge in a directory that others in the network can search? (required)
Best time to reach you
For example a phone call, email or WhatsApp message.
In your own time zone.
How we use your details
  • Your answers go to a Google Sheet. Only OpenSpeaks representatives, who organise the network, can open it.
  • This form sends your answers through Google Apps Script, a Google service. Google’s privacy policy also applies.
  • If we approve your request, we email you a member agreement as a PDF. We make it with Google Docs and send it with Gmail.
  • We use your details only for the network. We do not sell or share them.
  • Your details go in the network directory only if you say yes to that above.
  • To see, correct or remove your details, write to hello@archivingthepresent.cc.

Thank you

Your request has reached us. If you said yes, we will check your details and email you a member agreement. If you said maybe, we will contact you at the time you chose.
