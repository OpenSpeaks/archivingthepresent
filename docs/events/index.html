Events

Workshops, training series and summer schools on community-based language documentation and archiving, organised by OpenSpeaks with the Archiving the Present network.

Upcoming

    About seven days of in-person training in audio and video documentation and archiving of endangered languages, with trainers from the Endangered Languages Archive (ELAR) and OpenSpeaks.

  • Santali and Ho event

Past

  • Archiving the Present workshop, Kochi

    3 September 2026, Kochi

    The in-person launch of Archiving the Present, alongside WikiConference India. 34 people took part, including 19 community documenters and archivists who between them record and keep 27 languages and varieties.

  • Community Language Documentation and Archiving workshop series

    2026, online

    A three-month online workshop series with nearly 20 trainers from seven countries. It grew into the Archiving the Present manual.
