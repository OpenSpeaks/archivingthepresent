About the summer school

Participants spend about seven days in Bengaluru learning to record, describe and archive their languages in audio and video. Resource persons from language communities, Endangered Languages Archive (ELAR), People's Archive of Rural India (PARI), North Eastern Institute of Language and Culture (NEILAC), OpenSpeaks and other collaborators will conduct training. We plan to hold a summer school every year.

Collaborators

Who can apply

Documenters and archivists of endangered languages who are 18 or older. We'll bring together around 25 participants. Most places are for people living in India, and one or two are for people from elsewhere in South Asia. We give priority to languages that are endangered: languages with only a few thousand speakers or fewer, and that fewer children are learning at home. You don't need a degree or past documentation experience to apply.

What we cover

If you're selected, we cover your travel and stay. When the train or bus journey to Bengaluru would take more than 13 hours, we book a round-trip flight for you. For shorter journeys, we pay for your train or bus tickets. We also provide accommodation in Bengaluru for the whole summer school.

How we select

A small jury reads every application. Not every applicant can be part of this cohort, but we hope to stay connected with everyone through our online training and other capacity-building activities.

How to apply

Fill in the application form in Hindi or English by 18 December 2026.
